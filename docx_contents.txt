04. Why This Palette?
The Hubo shot uses a dark warm palette rather than the common:
black + Spotify green
approach. (Dribbble)
That's important.
If you use Spotify green everywhere, the product will visually feel like a Spotify companion app.
Instead:
ReelTune = warm amber identity
while Spotify/YouTube retain their own colors only when representing those integrations.
For example:
ReelTune → Amber
Spotify → Spotify Green
YouTube → YouTube Red
Instagram → Instagram Gradient
This creates a clear brand identity.

36. Final Visual Direction
If I were turning this into the actual Figma/Stitch design brief, I'd specify:
Create a premium dark-mode music discovery and playlist application inspired by the visual language of the Hubo AI Assistant App by Sam Halpert/Awsmd: warm near-black backgrounds, caramel/tan accents, oversized editorial typography, soft rounded surfaces, restrained UI, strong visual hierarchy and subtle motion. Do not replicate Hubo's layouts or branding. Adapt the aesthetic specifically for a Gen-Z music discovery product.
The visual identity should ultimately feel like:
Dark + Warm + Minimal + Musical + Premium
rather than:
Spotify clone + generic AI dashboard.
The Hubo reference's warm dark palette is particularly useful here because it gives your app an identity separate from Spotify's established visual language. (Dribbble)
Recommended screen sequence for the first prototype
Onboarding → Home → Instagram Save → Song Recognition → Saved Song → Library → Playlist → Spotify Sync → Profile
That gives you a complete end-to-end prototype rather than just a collection of attractive screens.

Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Product Name
Working Name: ReelTuneTagline: Discover it on Reels. Save it. Play it anywhere.
The name is provisional and can be changed later.

1. Product Overview
1.1 Problem Statement
People discover a huge number of songs through Instagram Reels. A user may hear a song they love but often faces friction:
They don't know the song's name.
They don't want to manually search for it.
They forget which Reel contained the song.
Instagram's saved-post system isn't designed specifically for music discovery.
Even when they identify the song, they have to manually search for it again on Spotify or YouTube.
Users may want to organize discovered songs into different playlists.
Users may use different music platforms and want flexibility.
1.2 Proposed Solution
Build a music-discovery and playlist-management application that allows users to:
Save a song discovered on Instagram/Reels.
Automatically identify the song whenever technically possible.
Store the song in the user's personal library.
Create and manage playlists.
Search for songs manually.
Add songs to playlists.
Connect music services such as Spotify and YouTube.
Export/sync playlists to connected services.
Play songs through the appropriate music platform.

Core User Journey
Instagram Reel
      ↓
User taps Share
      ↓
"Save to ReelTune"
      ↓
ReelTune receives Reel/song information
      ↓
Song Identification
      ↓
Song added to Saved Songs
      ↓
User chooses Playlist
      ↓
Playlist created/updated
      ↓
Spotify / YouTube
      ↓
Playlist synced

2. Product Vision
Vision
Create the easiest way to turn music discovered on social media into organized personal playlists.
Product Philosophy
Discover anywhere. Save instantly. Organize once. Listen anywhere.
The app should remove the annoying process of:
Hear song → remember lyrics → search Google → find song → search Spotify → add song → create playlist
and replace it with:
Hear song → Save → Done.

3. Target Users
Primary Users
Gen Z / Young Adults
Users who frequently:
Watch Instagram Reels.
Discover new music through social media.
Listen to Spotify or YouTube.
Create playlists.
Save songs based on mood, vibe, occasion, etc.
Example User
Rahul, 21
He watches Instagram Reels while travelling.
He hears:
"Bro this song is actually fire."
Normally he would:
Screenshot the Reel
Forget about it
Later search for the song
Maybe never find it
With the app:
Share → ReelTune → Saved.

4. Goals
Primary Goals
G1 — Frictionless Song Saving
Allow users to save a discovered song with as few actions as possible.
G2 — Automatic Song Identification
Identify songs from supported Reel/share information or user-provided audio whenever possible.
G3 — Personal Music Library
Maintain a centralized collection of songs discovered by the user.
G4 — Playlist Creation
Allow users to organize songs into custom playlists.
Examples:
Gym
Late Night
College
Road Trip
Hindi Hits
English
Chill

Reels Songs
G5 — Multi-Platform Support
Allow users to connect supported music services.
Initial target:
Spotify
YouTube / YouTube Music, subject to API capabilities and permissions
G6 — Playlist Synchronization
Allow users to export or create equivalent playlists on connected services.

5. Non-Goals for MVP
To keep the first version realistic, the MVP should not attempt to:
Become a replacement for Spotify.
Host copyrighted music.
Stream full copyrighted songs directly from our servers.
Download songs illegally.
Scrape Instagram aggressively.
Build a complete social network.
Build a recommendation engine immediately.
Support every music streaming platform from day one.
Automatically process every Instagram Reel without platform/API limitations.
The app should primarily function as a music discovery, saving, organization and playlist-sync layer.
